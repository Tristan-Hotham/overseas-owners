## 150-word summary for a cover letter

Debates about offshore ownership of British property focus on prime central London and a few national totals. This paper
maps the whole HM Land Registry register of titles held by overseas companies (November 2026; 90,949 titles) for every small
area of England and Wales and relates it to the Council Tax housing stock. Following an analysis plan written before any
counts were computed, it shows that the titles are far more concentrated than homes (Gini 0.87 against 0.14), yet present in
39% of small areas; that 76% are held by companies from the UK's own Crown Dependencies and Overseas Territories, more so
outside London; and that within districts they are more common where more homes are in the top Council Tax bands, are flats,
and were built since 2009. The results hold across every planned check and a within-district placebo. The paper also
releases small-area aggregates that others can reuse without handling the restricted title-level file.
